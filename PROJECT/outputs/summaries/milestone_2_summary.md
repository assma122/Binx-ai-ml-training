# Milestone 2 — Exploratory Data Analysis & Statistics

- The target was fairly balanced: 164 no-heart-disease cases and 139 heart-disease cases.
- Heart disease cases represented about 45.9% of the dataset.
- The main numerical features were age, trestbps, chol, thalach, and oldpeak.
- The strongest positive target correlations were `thal` (~0.516) and `ca` (~0.460).
- `thalach` had the strongest negative target correlation (~-0.417).
- IQR outlier counts were: age 0, trestbps 9, chol 5, thalach 1, oldpeak 5.
- Potential outliers were kept because they may represent valid patient measurements.
- Categorical plots showed noticeable differences for cp, exang, ca, and thal.
