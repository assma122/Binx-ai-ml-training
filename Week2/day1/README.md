# Day 1 - Descriptive Statistics

## Overview

In this day, I learned the basics of descriptive statistics and how they help summarize and understand data.

I studied measures of central tendency and measures of spread, then applied them to a real dataset.

## Topics Covered

### Measures of Central Tendency

- Mean
- Median
- Mode

### Measures of Spread

- Range
- Variance
- Standard deviation
- Quartiles
- Interquartile range (IQR)

## Hands-On Lab

I used the `insurance.csv` dataset and selected the `charges` column.

I calculated:

- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard deviation
- Q1 and Q3
- IQR

I found that the median represents a typical medical charge better than the mean because some high charge values increase the average.
